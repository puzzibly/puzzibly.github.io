How it plays

Sort it, then see why

  1. Read the cards. Each one is a clue.
  2. Put them in order. Drag, or tap two to swap.
  3. See the answer. The real dates and numbers, and up to 3 stars.
  4. Play your way. The Daily, rooms with friends, Ranked seasons, Card Clash.
Card Clash

Pick your stat, win the cards

A card battle with real facts. Choose the stat you think beats your rival's card, top speed, height, year, population, and take their card if you're right. Best of 3 rounds.
