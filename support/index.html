Puzzibly Support

Need help with Puzzibly? Email us at puzzibly.studio@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

To help us look into a problem, please include:

  • your game ID, found in Settings > About (tap Copy to copy it);
  • your phone or tablet model and the app version, shown at the bottom of Settings;
  • what you were doing when the problem happened.

You can also write to us from the app with Settings > About > Send Feedback.

Common questions

How do I restore Puzzibly Premium on a new phone?

Open Settings > Premium (or the Premium screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Premium with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them from. On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play app, then Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage Subscription in Puzzibly under Settings > Premium. Cancel at least 24 hours before your current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one.

Why did my progress reset on a new phone?

Your XP, coins, streaks, badges and settings are stored on your device, and there is no account to sign in to. If you restore Premium on the new device, your stats, rankings and match history move to your new game ID.

How do I report an offensive player or group name?

Email us with the name, where you saw it (for example the room, group or leaderboard), and your game ID. We will remove names that break our rules. Names with offensive words are also blocked when they are entered.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Go to Settings > Notifications & Privacy > Remind Me to Play. You can also turn notifications off in your device's settings.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. To delete the information stored on our servers, email us with your game ID. See our Privacy Policy for details.
