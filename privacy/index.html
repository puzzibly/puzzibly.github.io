Puzzibly Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Puzzibly app ("Puzzibly", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. Puzzibly is made by iota.

The short version

  • You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, never through us.
  • Your progress (XP, coins, streaks, badges, settings) lives on your device.
  • To run multiplayer, Ranked Match, Groups, and stats features, we store a random game ID with your game results on our servers.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app; you can turn this off in Settings.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not sell your information.

Information stored on your device

Puzzibly keeps most of your data only on your device: your progress, coins, cosmetics, badges, streaks, settings, puzzle history for the Continue and Next Puzzle features, and a copy of the puzzle library. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Information we collect

A random game ID

The first time you play, the app creates a random ID on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any account. We use it to connect your results to features such as rankings and stats. The app also creates a random secret that stays on your device; our servers keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy, to check that requests for your game ID really come from your device. If you reinstall the app or change devices, you get a new ID and start fresh.

Your player name

If you set a player name (Settings > Profile), it is stored on our servers with your game ID and shown to other players in multiplayer rooms, Ranked Match, Groups, and leaderboards. Please do not use your real full name or other personal details as your player name.

Game results

When you use these features, we store your results with your game ID:

  • Multiplayer rooms and Ranked Match: the puzzle played, your placement, correctness, moves, time, mistakes, hints used, and rating changes.
  • Card Clash: match results, rounds won and lost, the decks played, and rewards earned.
  • Ranked Match seasons: your rating, division, points, and season results.
  • Groups: the groups you create or join, their names and join codes, and when members last played.
  • Extended Stats: your solo puzzle results (puzzle, correctness, moves, time).
  • Badge rarity: which badges you have unlocked and when, used to show how rare a badge is ("X% of players have this").

Your region

For Regional ranked matches, the app reads a country or region code (for example, "US") from your device's language and region settings. We do not use GPS or your precise location.

Usage statistics

To understand which features are used and to fix crashes, the app sends anonymous events such as "puzzle started", "puzzle completed", "hint used", and "achievement unlocked", along with the app version and a separate random analytics ID. When the app crashes, it sends a technical error report. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Share Anonymous Usage Data.

Our multiplayer server also records match events (for example, players disconnecting and reconnecting, and ranked results) with your game ID, to keep multiplayer working reliably. When you turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data, the server leaves your game ID out of these events. Room-wide counts that contain no game ID (for example, "a room was created" or "a round finished with 4 players") are still recorded.

Notifications

If you turn on Remind Me to Play, the app schedules reminders on your device. If you also play Ranked Match or Groups, we store a push notification token with your game ID so we can tell you about season results and group updates. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in your device's settings.

Camera

The app uses your camera only to scan a room's QR code to join a game. The camera image is not saved or sent anywhere.

Messages you send us

If you email us (for example with Settings > Send Feedback), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and to improve Puzzibly.

Technical information

Like any app that connects to the internet, the services we use receive your device's IP address and basic technical details when the app makes a request. Our analytics provider may use the IP address to estimate a rough country or city for aggregate statistics.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • run multiplayer rooms, Ranked Match, Card Clash, Groups, seasons, and leaderboards;
  • show your stats, badges, and badge rarity;
  • send notifications you have turned on;
  • understand how the app is used, fix problems, and improve Puzzibly.

We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Who we share information with

We use these service providers to run Puzzibly, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database and puzzle library)
  • Colyseus Cloud (multiplayer game server)
  • PostHog (usage statistics and crash reports)
  • Expo (push notification delivery)
  • RevenueCat (purchase status for Puzzibly Premium; see Purchases below)
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store (app distribution and payments)

Other players see your player name, rankings, and game results in multiplayer and leaderboards, and a supporter badge next to your name if you have had Puzzibly Premium. We may also disclose information if required by law.

These providers may store and process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and Singapore.

How long we keep information

We keep game results, rankings, and stats for as long as the features that use them are offered, or until you ask us to delete them. We may delete older history to keep the service running smoothly. Groups that stay inactive are removed automatically after a warning period.

Your choices and rights

  • Usage statistics: turn off Settings > Share Anonymous Usage Data.
  • Notifications: turn off Remind Me to Play in Settings, or notifications in your device's settings.
  • Player name: change it at any time in Settings.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. To delete the information stored on our servers, email puzzibly.studio@gmail.com with your game ID, found in Settings > About (tap Copy to copy it). We then delete your game results, stats, rankings, badges, player name, and notification token. Groups you own pass to the member who has been in them longest, or close if you are the only member. We keep the records of your purchases, because the app stores and tax rules require them.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information, or to object to its use. Contact us to use any of these rights.

Children

Puzzibly's content is suitable for ages 7 and up, but the app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Purchases

Puzzibly Premium (a subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase) is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store handles payment; we never see or store your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives your game ID, the product you bought, purchase and renewal dates, and the store's purchase receipt, which may include your country and currency. Our servers may keep a record of which game IDs have Premium, so features such as Groups can apply Premium limits, and a record that your game ID has supported Puzzibly, for the supporter badge.

If you restore your purchase on a new device, we may move the game results stored with your old game ID (for example, your stats, ranking, and match history) to your new game ID so you keep them.

Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Google Play or App Store account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
