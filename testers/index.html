Tester guide

Thank you for helping us test Puzzibly before launch. The test plan has 12 days of scenarios, about 15 minutes a day.

Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google only lets us publish Puzzibly after testers have stayed in the closed test for 14 days in a row. Keep Puzzibly installed and don't leave the test, even after Day 12, until we say in the group chat that the 14 days are over. Playing a little on days 13 and 14 helps too.

Your number and group were sent to you when you joined. Your day plan appears below.

Before Day 1

Android: open the test invite link on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install Puzzibly from Google Play. Purchases in the test are free test purchases; you won't be charged.

iPhone or iPad: install Apple's TestFlight app, open the invite we emailed you, and install Puzzibly from TestFlight. Purchases use a test (sandbox) account; you won't be charged.

Join the testers' group chat: it's where we agree the times for the two group sessions and share Groups codes.

Each day

  1. Open today's plan below and do the Required scenarios. They take about 15 minutes.
  2. If you have time, try your Bonus scenario.
  3. Report anything that looks wrong, as soon as you see it (see below).
  4. When you're done, send the "I finished today's scenarios" report.

Missed a day? Do it the next day, before that day's plan. Days 4 and 9 are group sessions at an agreed time; if you can't make it, tell us in the chat.

Known issues

Samsung phones: the app looks too big or blurry. Samsung may treat Puzzibly as a game and lower its resolution, so everything looks oversized (sometimes only the first time you open it).

  1. Close Puzzibly fully (swipe it away from recent apps) and open it again.
  2. If it still looks too big: open Game Launcher (or Gaming Hub), find Puzzibly in your library, press and hold it and choose Remove. Then close and reopen Puzzibly.

Since build 20 the app restarts itself once when this happens, so you may see it close and reopen right after an update. If it still looks too big after that, please report it with your phone model.
