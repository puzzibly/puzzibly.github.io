PUZZIBLY

Tester guide

Thank you for helping us test Puzzibly before launch. The test runs for 12 days, about 15 minutes a day.

Your number and group were sent to you when you joined. Your day plan appears below.

Before Day 1

Android: open the test invite link on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install Puzzibly from Google Play. Purchases in the test are free test purchases; you won't be charged.

iPhone or iPad: install Apple's TestFlight app, open the invite we emailed you, and install Puzzibly from TestFlight. Purchases use a test (sandbox) account; you won't be charged.

Join the testers' group chat: it's where we agree the times for the two group sessions and share Groups codes.

Each day

  1. Open today's plan below and do the Required scenarios. They take about 15 minutes.
  2. If you have time, try your Bonus scenario.
  3. Report anything that looks wrong, as soon as you see it (see below).
  4. When you're done, send the "I finished today's scenarios" report.

Missed a day? Do it the next day, before that day's plan. Days 4 and 9 are group sessions at an agreed time; if you can't make it, tell us in the chat.
